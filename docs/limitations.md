# Limitations

- Discovery and popularity do not establish truth or expertise. Sources may be wrong, duplicated, outdated, or unavailable; conflict absence does not prove agreement.
- Public queries and source fetches need network access. A connector failure does not mean the outside world lacks relevant work. Rate limits and source structure changes can interrupt research.
- YouTube captions may be missing or inaccurate; media downloads may fail. ASR quality depends on language and recording conditions. Speaker diarization and identity need separate evidence. A changed frame is not automatically a meaningful visual claim.
- Audio, frame, and dedicated-browser paths have macOS-specific confinement and optional dependencies. They are not a portable, automatically installed media stack. Model weights and source media are not shipped.
- A long research job may take minutes. Disk, memory, and quota guards may stop it. No background watcher runs by default.
- An intermittent native Python import error has previously been observed but could not be reproduced reliably. Diagnostic receipts are retained for future occurrences; no import-race fix is claimed.
- Health and replacement contracts are experimental. They do not provide production-proven self-healing or automatic upgrades.
- A local extractive dossier is research material, not an accepted decision or permission to change another system.
