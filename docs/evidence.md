# Evidence and dossier

Evidence is a versioned record bound to a research question and source revision. It records a source locator, captured and published time when known, claim, provenance artifact hash, acquisition form, confidence, relevance, novelty, impact, and limitations. A transcript locator can include a video time; a frame needs the source video ID, timestamp, and frame hash. Description-linked resources preserve the parent video relationship.

The local `data/discovery/<job>/evidence.json` contains extractive, located snippets; `dossier.md` is a readable draft over those materials. `result.json` records counts and limits. A source assertion is not independent verification. Same-origin copies do not count as independent corroboration, and a real conflict remains unresolved until a located experiment or review can settle it. Do not silently remove failures, uncertain text, or missing sources.
